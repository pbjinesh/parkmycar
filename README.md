# Park My Car

Parked in a big mall and can't remember where? Tap **Park here**, and find your car again later.

## Features
- One-tap save of GPS location, time, floor, slot or pillar number, note and photo
- Warns when GPS is weak (basements) so you rely on floor, slot and photo
- **Find my car**: distance, direction arrow and Google Maps walking directions
- Edit, share and clear the saved spot, plus a short history
- 100% on-device: no account, no server, no tracking

## Try it
Live demo: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/park-my-car/
Open on your phone in Chrome, then menu > Add to Home screen.

## Run locally
cd www
python -m http.server 8000
Open http://localhost:8000 and allow location.
