# Privacy Policy – Park My Car

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Park My Car helps you remember where you parked your vehicle.

**What we collect.** When you tap "Park here", the app reads your device's location (GPS) once, and any floor, slot number, note and photo you choose to add.

**Where it is stored.** All of this stays on your device only. The app has no account, no server and no analytics. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data.

**Location.** Location is used only to save your parking spot and to show the distance and direction back to it while the "Find my car" screen is open. The app does not track you in the background.

**Photos.** The camera is used only when you choose "Take photo". Photos are stored on your device inside the app.

**Sharing.** If you tap "Share", the text you choose to send goes to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). Directions open in Google Maps, which has its own privacy policy.

**Deleting data.** Tap "I've left – clear spot", or uninstall the app, to remove saved data.
